GroovyScript adds the ability to craft things in-world via specific processes. By default, no recipes are created and everything must be added by you through GroovyScript.

## Categories

Has 6 subcategories.

* [Burning Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/burning/)

* [Explosion Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/explosion/)

* [Fluid and ItemStack To Block Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/fluid_to_block/)

* [Fluid and ItemStack To Fluid Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/fluid_to_fluid/)

* [Fluid and ItemStack To ItemStack Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/fluid_to_item/)

* [Piston Pushing Conversion](/groovy-script/minecraft/in_world_crafting/piston_push/)
